## Concepto

El isotipo conecta tres ideas:

- hojas y crecimiento;
- circuito como tronco/raíces;
- horizonte u ola costera.

La silueta se simplificó respecto de la plantilla original para mejorar la lectura en móvil.

## Prompt del isotipo

~~~text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: master brand isotype for website, favicon and social avatar
Input images: Image 1 is a visual style and concept reference only; do not crop or copy it literally
Primary request: create an original, reformulated symbol for Hola Mundo Verde that preserves the reference idea of nature plus technology but is much simpler, cleaner and scalable
Subject: a strong circular silhouette combining a small tree or sprout canopy in the upper half, a circuit-like trunk and roots in the center, and one subtle coastal wave or horizon curve in the lower half; the parts must read as one unified mark
Style/medium: flat vector-friendly logo mark, geometric organic shapes, consistent bold strokes, crisp edges, balanced negative space
Composition/framing: one single centered isotype with generous transparent padding, perfectly upright and symmetrical enough for favicon use
Color palette: forest #073B2A, green #0B5D42, leaf #2E8B57, small lime accent #BCEB67 and optional ocean #4B8FA8; maximum four flat colors
Background: genuinely transparent
Constraints: no text, no initials, no extra logo variations, no mockup, no shadow, no 3D, no photographic texture, no watermark, no thin micro-details; readable at 24 px and valid in monochrome
Avoid: generic leaf inside a circle, realistic tree, globe map, recycling symbol, light bulb, brain circuitry, copied reference details, gradients
~~~

Se aplicó una segunda iteración para reducir hojas, unificar la base costera y preparar una extracción de fondo con canal alfa real.

## Prompt del lockup horizontal

~~~text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: horizontal website logo lockup
Input images: Image 1 is the approved isotype reference; preserve its concept and colors, do not redesign it
Primary request: create one clean horizontal logo lockup with the isotype on the left and an exact two-line typographic wordmark on the right
Text (verbatim): first line "Hola Mundo Verde"; second line "GreenTech Innovation Lab"
Typography: modern humanist geometric sans serif similar in spirit to Manrope, bold first line and small medium-weight second line with modest letter spacing; exact spelling and capitalization, no extra characters
Composition/framing: wide horizontal layout, vertically centered, generous transparent padding, symbol height aligned to the two text lines
Color palette: wordmark forest #073B2A, subtitle green #0B5D42; preserve isotype colors
Background: genuinely transparent
Constraints: exactly one lockup, exact text only once, no extra leaf after the text, no tagline, no mockup, no shadow, no 3D, no border, no watermark
Avoid: curved text, decorative font, condensed font, tiny unreadable subtitle, gradients in the wordmark
~~~

## Reglas de uso

- No deformar ni cambiar proporciones.
- Dejar aire equivalente al ancho del nodo central.
- No usar el lockup horizontal por debajo de 180 px de ancho.
- En tamaños pequeños usar el isotipo.
- No convertir este PNG en master de impresión.
- Validar la marca en monocromo antes de cerrar el SVG definitivo.
- Realizar búsqueda de similitud y revisión legal antes de registrar.
